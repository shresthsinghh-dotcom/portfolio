Research · Volume

Estimating Mine-Waste Volumes from Elevation Data

Duke Data+ 2026 · Critical Materials

The value of a dump is, roughly, Value = Volume × Grade × Price × Recovery, and volume is the term that is missing at scale, because while the inventory gives footprints, it rarely gives depths. I built a pipeline to supply that one number — volume — from remote elevation data alone: reconstructing the buried ground beneath a dump with a thin-plate spline fit to the surrounding terrain, then subtracting it from the exterior surface. Benchmarked against USGS field volumes across 100 sites in four states, the result is more honest and more useful than tuning: a fully characterized method with a stated domain of validity, and a rigorous account of exactly where and why it fails.

Research · Grade

Tungsten Queen Mine Geochemistry Analysis

Duke Data+ 2026 · Companion single-site study

As part of my Duke Data+ research work, I analyzed a public geochemistry dataset from the Tungsten Queen Mine, an abandoned tungsten mine and critical-mineral tailings site in North Carolina. This exploratory notebook cleaned below-detection-limit values, summarized elemental concentrations, mapped sample locations, and visualized tungsten patterns across depth, sample type, and correlated elements. The project served as a compact research-computing exercise in Python, combining data cleaning, statistical comparison, geospatial visualization, and scientific interpretation.

Tooling · FEA

Automating Formula SAE Load-Case Analysis

Duke Motorsports 2026 · Vehicle Dynamics

Sizing the front uprights meant running 28 load cases through the team's suspension-force spreadsheet and reading the resulting member forces back out — slow by hand, and easy to get wrong halfway through. I wrote a script that drives Excel directly as a calculation engine: it writes each case's lateral and longitudinal accelerations into the model, waits for the workbook to finish recalculating, reads the per-corner forces, and collects everything into a single clean output workbook. I validated both the script and its numbers against the previous year's load cases before anything downstream depended on them.

Tool · Geospatial

Satellite & Environmental Data Analysis

Interactive Streamlit application

Satellite rasters carry more than any single image shows — surface temperature and vegetation cover have to be computed from the bands rather than observed directly. This tool builds that workflow end to end: it loads real GeoTIFF datasets, smooths the imagery to suppress pixel-level noise, derives land surface temperature and NDVI, and tests how the two relate within a selected region. Wrapping it in Streamlit makes the parameters adjustable, so the effect of each choice is visible immediately instead of buried in a notebook cell.
