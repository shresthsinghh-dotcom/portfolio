Perspective

Beyond the Resume

While my academic and technical work reflects how I approach problem-solving, my philosophical and creative pursuits reflect why I approach problems the way I do.

These pursuits shape how I reason about systems, responsibility, meaning, and long-term consequences — questions that extend beyond any single discipline.

Together, they offer a more complete picture of my development as both a student and an individual.

Philosophical Orientation

My philosophical interests center on questions of agency, responsibility, and how individuals construct meaning within complex systems. I draw on Stoic ethics, existentialist thought, and systems-level reasoning to examine how intention translates into action — and how the frameworks we adopt shape the consequences we produce.

Music as Reflection

This ongoing project has sharpened my attention, reflection, and appreciation for structure, variation, and creative intent across genres — from hip-hop and metal to R&B, rock, and film scoring.
