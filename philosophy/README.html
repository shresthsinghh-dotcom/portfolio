The Library

Our existence troubles us. The mind, that bright spark, possesses the unique ability to understand reality, yet that same reality extinguishes it so quickly. Coming to terms with this short existence that is the search for wisdom. To be wise is to live the best life. Though we know not the right path, the attempt to find it must be closer to wisdom than ignorance. Thus we embark upon this journey.

The goal is to traverse over 2,600 years of knowledge gathered from the central strongholds of thought: the West and the East. We begin with the West, covering nearly a millennium and concluding upon the wane of the Roman Empire’s golden philosophical age with its last great emperor, Marcus Aurelius.
