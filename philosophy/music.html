My Journey Through Music

On February 1st, 2025, I my life changed forever. After listening to one of the greatest albums in hip hop history, I realized that too much beautiful music was out there, and it was my duty to myself to change that. I made a decision. I would listen to one new album a day until I ran out of good music (I doubt this will ever happen!) Thus began my quest.

Below is this journey, documented. The artists I have already listened, as well as the artists I plan to listen to. Within, I will do my best to categorize them by genre. Eventually, I plan to take the artists most special to me and analyze their entire discography.
