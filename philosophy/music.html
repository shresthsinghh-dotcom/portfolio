My Journey Through Music

On February 1st, 2025, my life changed forever. After listening to one of the greatest albums in hip-hop history, I realized that too much beautiful music was out there, and it was my duty to myself to change that. I made a decision: I would listen to one new album a day until I ran out of good music. I doubt this will ever happen.

Below is this journey, documented — the artists I have already heard, as well as those I plan to hear. Eventually, I plan to take the artists most special to me and analyze their entire discography.
