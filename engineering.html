My Engineering

My engineering work focuses on human-centered mechanical design, prototyping, and iterative testing. I am particularly interested in mechanical systems that balance functionality, safety, and accessibility under real-world constraints.

Through course-based and team-based projects, I have applied the engineering design process from early problem definition through fabrication and validation. My work emphasizes structured decision-making, tolerance-aware design, and continuous refinement based on testing and feedback.

Adaptive Ball Launcher for Accessible Independent Play

Project Team

This is the team I built this project with! We worked together to design and build the adaptive ball launcher in EGR 101, a class focused around engineering design and communication.

Low-Fidelity Prototype

Once we discussed our design criteria with our clients, we decided to build a low-fidelity prototype to test our initial concepts and gather feedback early in the design process. What you see is a very basic trebutchet-style launcher made from cardboard and rubber bands. We quickly realized that this design wouldn't be very safe for use with our clients.

Medium-Fidelity Prototype

We then built a medium-fidelity prototype using a more robust frame and a spring-loaded mechanism, which allowed us to test the launch force and trajectory more accurately. We liked this design a lot more, and used this framework for the high-fidelity design.

Final Prototype

This is the final prototype, fully integrated with a modular frame and low-force activation. It uses an Arduino curcuit to turn a linear drive, which allows a user to lauch the the ball with minimal force.
