Philosophical Orientation

In search of the truth, we have thought a great deal. To be wise is to make the best decisions, and to live the best life. I do not know the right path; all I know is that the attempt to find it must be closer to wisdom than ignorance. Thus I embark upon this journey.

The goal is to traverse over 2,500 years of knowledge gathered from three central strongholds of thought: the West, the East, and the South. We begin with the West, covering nearly a millennium and concluding upon the wane of the Roman Empire with its last great emperor, Marcus Aurelius.
