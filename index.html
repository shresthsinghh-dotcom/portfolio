Hello, I'm

Shresth Singh

Welcome. I'm a mechanical engineering student at Duke. From carbon-fiber airframes, Formula SAE suspension components, to analytical work, I build things that work, and prove it. This summer, I researched critical materials with Duke's Data+ program, betting on a future I'm fairly serious about: the coming AI buildout will surge energy demand, and so it will require physical systems that work.

Everything here is progress toward that same goal: whether it's my engineering, programming, leadership, or a long-running reading project in philosophy.
